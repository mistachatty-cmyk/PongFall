## What PongFall is
Pong in a constantly falling arena. Each side is behind glass that breaks after 3 missed balls (no repair); after a break, a ball through the hologram goal line is an auto-score. Walls have eyes (metal lids): one hit opens them all and drops a tear. Scoring grows your arena (slows the fall); the opponent scoring shrinks it. Ends on target score, time, or the arena wedging against both walls. LOK account sync and Hub discovery are supported. Match results remain unranked and grant no LokTokens.

## Gotchas
- **Never use the word "signal"** anywhere (CLAUDE.md rule).
- Any change that alters how a recorded run plays out must bump `SIM_VERSION`; `replay.test.ts` guards determinism. Render-only state must never feed the sim.
- Test script quoting matters: `"test": "tsx --test \"src/**/*.test.ts\""`.
- Clones here are shallow/single-branch; if a git hook says "no remote branch", fetch with `+refs/heads/<b>:refs/remotes/origin/<b>`.
- Do not `pkill -f` a pattern that appears in your own command line (it kills the shell).
- Sibling game: `mistachatty-cmyk/Kinetic-Souls` (4k-line single file, mock LOK passport); borrow ideas (modifiers, unlimited mode, themes), not structure. Full plan lives in the session plan and `CLAUDE.md`'s milestone list.

## 0.10.0 online multiplayer (SIM_VERSION unchanged: 14)
- Lockstep netcode, `src/net/`: `lockstep.ts` (session, 20-tick input delay, 6-tick batches, state hash every 120 ticks, epochs for rematches), `room.ts` (host/join by code), `supabaseChannel.ts` (Supabase Realtime Broadcast on LokServices, nothing stored, supabase-js is loaded on demand), `memoryChannel.ts` (test double). - Needs `VITE_SUPABASE_URL` and `VITE_SUPABASE_ANON_KEY` at build time: the public LokServices values are committed in `.env.production` (Vercel's env settings could not be read or written from here). - The guest plays side 1 with their own p1 keys (`ME` in `createPongFall.ts`); the guest does not collect or slice tears (tears feed the host's pinball arm). A desync shows "out of sync" and ends the match.
- Pause menu gained `pauseExtras()` (info, sliders, toggles, key hints); online it does not pause.
