# Roadmap status

Plan: sides and layers, flags and album art, eyes and companions, menus and settings, multiplayer and social. Each phase ships as its own release.

## Phase 0: bugs and polish (0.27.0)
- Done: lok spelling rule and guard test, no "signal" in player-facing names, hints off by default, rotating menu flavor lines, rally metronome, HelloWorld cheat menu, other-arena bot names (toggle), smooth menu eye plus seven-poke easter egg.

## Phase 1: arena sides and layers (0.28.0)
- Done: `core/arenaSides.ts` (looks, sanitize, storage, ceiling blend, NPC presets), `render/sideLayers.ts` (layer 1 back glass + pictures, layer 2 objects, layer 4 filter), `core/centerLine.ts` (developer-only seasonal centre line), `core/artRegistry.ts` (own art with source and licence), `core/arenaSidesView.ts` (editor on a real running arena, plus whole-shaft view), main menu entry, cloud sync of looks, online `cos` message (render-only, size capped, sanitized, never in the lockstep hash).
- Live everywhere: your match, the menu backdrop rally, the shop venues, and the other arenas in the shaft (NPC presets).
- Not built yet: physical layer-2 objects (needs a `SIM_VERSION` bump and an online review, so it stays an experiment), per-NPC custom looks.
- 0.28.1: drag-to-place inside the live arena (pointer mapped through the arena's swaying transform via `Fx.arenaMatrix`).

## Phase 2: flags, images, album art, quips (0.29.0)
- Done: `scripts/import-flags.ts` (one command imports a whole flag-icons folder: SVGs to `public/art/flags/`, registry rows to `core/artFlags.generated.ts`), 249 ISO flags shipped with the MIT licence text, album cover entries (own Game Jam Playlist cover; each names its music-player `trackId`), click quips in the editor (album covers play and pause), profile picture slot (`core/profilePicture.ts`, own side only, never sent to opponents).
- Rule kept: own and licensed art only. Covers for other artists or classic games stay out until you clear each one (add the row to `ART` with `cleared: true` after clearing).
- Not built yet: clickable quips during a real match (pointer is used for tear slicing, needs a design call), creator/streamer/artist packs (the importer and registry are ready; they need the actual art and permissions), other game soundtracks as album rows (needs covers).

## Phase 3a: eyes and LilEye (0.30.0)
- Done: detailed default eyes (shaded sclera, iris fibres, limbal ring, highlights, glossy lids) with the classic flat eyes kept behind Settings; LilEye companion (`core/lilEye.ts` pure rules, `core/lilEyeView.ts` sprite, speech bubble and page) with 3 personalities, rename, iris colours, reactions to near misses, cracks, points, tears, win and fall, and an off switch; lokpet catalog SQL (`supabase/registry/pongfall-lokpet-catalog.sql`, generated by `scripts/export-lokpet-catalog.ts`, for you to apply).
- Not built yet: LilEye as a falling object in the shop arena, more personalities and costumes, LilEye feeding on tears, dex page with other LOK games' lokpets.
