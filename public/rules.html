Rules and how it works

Pong in an arena that never stops falling down a deep shaft. Hold your glass, score through theirs, and keep an eye on the dark. It is watching.

The goal

You are the left paddle, the NPC is the right. Win by reaching the target score (14 by default) before the other side does, or by having the higher score when the time runs out in Timed mode.

The whole arena is falling. The faster it falls, the louder the wind, and the more the world shakes. Growing your arena slows the fall.

The short version: bounce the ball back with your paddle. Every miss cracks your glass. When a glass is gone, a ball through that side scores for the other player.

Glass

Each side sits behind a pane of glass. When the ball gets past a paddle and hits the glass, that is a miss: the glass cracks. Every pane survives 3 misses and shatters on the next one. On Chill your glass survives one extra.

  • Cracks branch out from where the ball hit and stay on the pane. The pane shatters along those cracks.
  • Each side has its own counter ("GLASS 2" means two more misses and it breaks).
  • Glass cannot be repaired. Once it is gone, that side is open.
Open side: a ball that crosses the dashed hologram line of an open side is an automatic point for the other player.

A ball that leaves through an open side flies out into the shaft. It can bounce off the walls and come back in.

Scoring and falling

  • You score: your arena grows, and the fall slows down.
  • They score: your arena shrinks a little, and the fall picks up.
  • Every score gives the fall a short surge and shakes the arena.
  • If the arena grows until it is wedged against both walls, the fall grinds to a halt and the game ends.

The ball speeds up a little with every paddle hit. Where the ball meets your paddle sets its angle: hit it near an edge for a sharp angle.

Thrust

A short lunge of your paddle toward the other side, with real momentum. The speed of the lunge is handed to the ball when you hit it, so a well-timed thrust is a fast return. After using it, it recharges (see the small bar under the score).

Eyes and tears

The shaft walls are lined with eyes behind metal lids. Plain wall just bounces the ball. An eye reacts:

  • Hit a wall eye with the ball and every eye in the wall opens for a while. The one you hit squashes, makes a wet sound, and drops a tear.
  • Tears are big and slow, and the wind blows them around. Slice through them (drag or swipe) to collect them. You can hold up to 4.
  • The eyes watch the ball, flinch when hit, and look shocked when glass breaks.

Far back in the dark there are fainter eyes. They glow when the wall eye next to them is hit. Later in a game they give way to something much bigger.

Something is watching. Past the halfway point, one huge eye appears deep in the shaft. Its pair is one eye on the opposite wall. Hit that wall eye and the huge eye opens. What it is has not been explained yet.

Power Paddle

  • Unlock: fill the tear meter (4 tears) for the first time. It stays unlocked in this browser. In Sandbox you start with it.
  • Activate: press E, tap the POWER button, or tap with two fingers. It spends 4 tears and lasts 12 seconds.
  • What it does: the paddle keeps real velocity, and every third hit is a boosted strike: extra ball speed, a flash, and a shove through the whole arena. The counter on your paddle shows how close the next boost is.

Comeback rules

Second wind: if the NPC leads by 4 or more, your Power Paddle switches on by itself until you lead by 2.
The depths claim the paddle: if the NPC leads by 6 or more, the NPC gets the Power Paddle for the rest of the game, and your second wind never comes back, even if you fight your way back.
Perk, "TheGreatsAreNeverForgotten": win a game after the NPC reached that 6-point lead and you earn it. It is a badge only. It does not make anything easier.

These rules do not apply in Sandbox, and on Relentless there is no second wind (the 6-point lock still applies).

Difficulty

Chill default
Slow NPC, slower ball, a longer paddle and an extra glass for you. Made for a first run.
Casual
The NPC gets a few good returns in.
Sharp
It reads the bounce and lunges.
Relentless
Rarely misses. No second wind. Bring thrust.

Change it any time in the gear menu (it restarts the game). Scores are kept separately for each difficulty.

Settings

Open the gear button. Everything is saved in this browser.

  • Game (restarts the game): difficulty, mode, score to win (7, 14, 21, 28 or the mode's own) and time limit (none, 1:00, 2:00, 3:00 or the mode's own).
  • Sound: master, effects and wind/ambience volume, and mute.
  • Graphics: fog quality (High uses a shader, Low is cheaper, Off removes it) and Reduce motion.
  • Controls and HUD: show or hide the control hints and the wind gauge, show or hide the on-screen THRUST and POWER buttons, and swap them for left-handed play.
  • Reset to defaults puts everything back.

Custom game lengths are kept apart from the standard ones when scores are ranked.

Tips

  • Start on Chill and try Sandbox to feel thrust before a real game.
  • Do not chase tears with your paddle: slice them in passing, between rallies.
  • Save your Power Paddle for when you are behind, or use it to close out a game.
  • A thrust right as the ball arrives hits much harder than a normal return.
  • Wind pushes tears sideways: slice with the wind, not against it.
  • Sensitive to motion? Turn on Reduce Motion in the gear menu.

Scores and rewards

Your score is points scored times 100, minus the other side's points times 40, plus a bonus for winning (and a speed bonus for winning fast). Your best is saved in this browser for each mode and difficulty.
